\begin{itshape}
    \textbf{Abstract:} \\

Automated Driving Systems (ADS) critically depend on robust perception systems capable of generating reliable environmental representations. Bird's-Eye View (BEV) maps are fundamental for this, as they offer a unified, top-down 2D representation of the vehicle's immediate surroundings. Traditional methods generate BEV semantic maps by first segmenting the perspective image and then applying Inverse Perspective Mapping (IPM). This process is referred to in this thesis as Segmenting-Then-IPM. However, the impact of directly training semantic segmentation models on BEV images remains unexplored.

This master's thesis analyzes whether training a semantic segmentation model directly on BEV images outperforms the traditional method, particularly for planar elements as they get a metric representation in the BEV domain. This alternative approach, referred to in this thesis as IPM-Then-Segmenting, reverses the order of the traditional strategy by first reprojecting the input image and then performing segmentation.

To enable a direct comparison between Segmenting-Then-IPM and IPM-Then-Segmenting, a novel BEV2Seg\_2 framework is developed. Contrary to the initial hypothesis of this thesis, experiments consistently demonstrated that the Segmenting-Then-IPM strategy yielded superior segmentation performance for planar elements. Additionally, this work found that modifying camera extrinsic parameters for BEV data augmentation was more effective than traditional geometric augmentations, significantly reducing model overfitting.

Beyond this comparative analysis, this work also explores a practical application by developing an automated pre-annotation pipeline to generate BEV masks for occupancy, occlusion, and driveable areas.  While this pipeline has some limitations, especially with partially occluded objects, it produces reliable enough masks for valuable downstream tasks in autonomous driving, such as scene reconstruction and path planning. 

    \textbf{Keywords: Automated Driving Systems (ADS), BEV Semantic Segmentation, Automated Annotation, Computer Vision, Occupancy Mapping, Occlusion Estimation}
\end{itshape}
